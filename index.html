OPTIONAL ABILITY ADJUSTMENT

Trade 2 points from Strength, Intelligence, or Wisdom to raise a prime requisite by 1. Dexterity, Constitution, and Charisma cannot be lowered; no score may be lowered below 9.

Read Magic is included automatically; the character can memorize one 1st-level spell per day at 1st level.

PARTY BEING FORMED

There is no party-size cap. Every adventurer added here receives a full XP share when the expedition later earns experience.
